## 1\. Project Overview

We propose to design and develop a complete, production-ready rental marketplace for RENDZO.

The platform will enable customers to browse rental products, select rental periods, check real-time availability, place bookings, make online payments and arrange delivery and pickup.

The platform will support two inventory sources:

1. **RENDZO-Owned Products:** Products directly owned and managed by RENDZO.  
2. **Third-Party Owner Products:** Products submitted by external owners and published after RENDZO administrator approval.

RENDZO will maintain centralized control over customer service, product approvals, booking management, delivery, pickup, commissions and financial records.

The website will be developed with a focus on reliability, security, maintainability, mobile responsiveness and future scalability.
