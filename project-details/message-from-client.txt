PROJECT: RENDZO Rental Website MVP

TAGLINE: Everything You Need. Nothing to Own.

LOCATION: Kochi, Kerala, India

BUDGET: ₹15,000–₹40,000

Project Objective

Develop a mobile-friendly rental website where customers can browse products, select rental periods, check availability, book online, make payments and arrange delivery and pickup.

RENDZO will manage customer service and logistics.

The platform must support two inventory sources:

1. Products owned by RENDZO.
2. Products listed by third-party owners and approved by the RENDZO admin.

Required Features

1. Responsive homepage and product catalogue.
2. Product categories, search and filtering.
3. Product details, rental pricing and deposits.
4. Rental date selection and availability checking.
5. Online booking and payment gateway integration.
6. Unique booking IDs and booking confirmations.
7. Customer accounts and booking history.
8. Admin dashboard.
9. Product and inventory management.
10. Booking and payment management.
11. Delivery and pickup scheduling.
12. Rental extensions and overdue tracking.
13. Third-party owner registration and product submissions.
14. Admin approval of owners and listings.
15. Owner commission and payout records.
16. Customer support and WhatsApp integration.
17. Email notifications.
18. Terms, privacy, cancellation and refund policy pages.
19. Basic SEO, analytics and website backups.

Important Business Rules

- Prevent overlapping confirmed bookings for the same physical product.
- Clearly show all rental charges before payment.
- Record refundable deposits separately from rental revenue.
- Verify payment status on the server.
- Require admin approval before third-party products become publicly available.
- Record product ownership, condition, maintenance and booking history.
- Allow manual management of delivery and pickup assignments.
- Keep a record of booking-status changes and financial transactions.

Preferred Development Approach

Use a maintainable e-commerce platform with a proven rental-booking solution wherever possible.

Avoid building complex features from scratch unless necessary.

Handover Requirements

Provide:

- Fully working website.
- Admin login.
- Domain and hosting access.
- Source code and configuration files as agreed.
- Payment-gateway integration documentation.
- Backup and recovery instructions.
- Basic SEO setup.
- Website administration training.
- Testing report.
- List of recurring costs and plugin licences.

Acceptance Criteria

The website will be considered ready only after successful testing of a complete customer booking, a failed payment, a rental return, an overlapping-booking attempt and a third-party product approval.

Please provide a fixed quotation, delivery timeline, technology stack, recurring costs and a list of features included within the agreed budget before development begins.
