How RENDZO works

Four simple steps from browsing to pickup

1

Pick your product & dates

Choose what you need and select rental start and end dates. See the full price and refundable deposit upfront.

2

Book & pay online

Pay securely via UPI, cards or net banking. You get a unique booking ID and confirmation instantly.

3

We deliver & install

Our team delivers to your doorstep in Kochi within 1–3 days and sets everything up for free.

4

Extend or return

Extend your rental in one tap, or schedule a free pickup. Your deposit is refunded after return.
